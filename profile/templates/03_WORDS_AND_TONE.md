# Words, tone, and writing guardrails

Last updated: 2026-01-03

## Tone
- Professional, calm, direct, human.
- Confident, but never arrogant.
- Explain complex things simply.
- Avoid corporate hype.

## Words to avoid (initial list)
- spearheading
- world-class
- cutting-edge
- disruptive
- rockstar, ninja, guru
- synergies
- leverage (as a verb, unless truly needed)
- fast-paced environment (unless quoting the job post)

## Preferred alternatives
- led
- owned
- built
- delivered
- improved
- shipped
- simplified
- reduced
- standardized
- stabilized
- introduced
- migrated

## Formatting
- No em dashes.
- Prefer bullets over long paragraphs.
